Spear at the start · weaponless Hollow Palm from 23 · Tempest Flurry + Staggering Palm in endgame — Ronarray's guide explainedThe Hyper Speed Monk

From 1 to 23 you play with a spear (3× Whirling Slash and one Twister). Between 23 and 25 the Hollow Palm Technique node takes the weapon out of the equation: you attack empty-handed and your speed grows with the Evasion on your gear. In endgame that's 12 to 18 attacks per second with Tempest Flurry and Staggering Palm, and Hollow Focus's bells generate the Power Charges Falling Thunder needs. Tell it your level and what you have: the guide shows what to do now, gem by gem, item by item.
